## Context

The client had committed to launching a new analytics platform across several markets. Internal hiring could not move fast enough, and the programme timeline could not slip.

## Approach

We agreed a skills matrix with the client's data leadership, then sourced against it in waves, so the first engineers could shape the platform foundations before the wider team arrived.

Every engineer was technically assessed by a Winvigor practitioner before introduction. Onboarding followed the client's own standards, and the client's delivery leads directed the work from day one.

## Outcome

- A senior data engineering team in place in time for the platform launch.
- Engineers working to the client's standards and tooling from their first week.
- Continuity for the client's own leadership, without a long permanent-hiring cycle.
