## Context

The client had committed to launching a new analytics platform across its European markets. Internal hiring could not move fast enough, and the programme could not slip.

## Approach

We agreed a skills matrix with the client's head of data, then sourced against it in three waves so the first engineers could shape the platform foundations before the larger team arrived.

Every engineer was technically assessed by a Winvigor practitioner before introduction. Onboarding followed the client's own standards, and the client's delivery leads directed the work from day one.

## Outcome

- Twenty-two engineers in place within twelve weeks.
- The platform launched on its original date.
- Ninety-five percent of the team were still in place a year later, giving the client continuity it could not have built through contractors alone.
